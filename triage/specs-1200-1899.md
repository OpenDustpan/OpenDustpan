Second triage pass over the open issues numbered 1200 to 1899 (163 issues, pull requests excluded). Each issue body was read, and each claim about main was checked with `git grep` against the checkout at origin main.

Classes:

- CHEAP: mechanical and periphery-safe. Excludes pkg/namespace, pkg/identity, pkg/blob, pkg/secret, the namespace fence, pkg/dustpan/dustpan.go, the baseline SQL schema files, internal/api/apidesc/openapi.json, and authentication, authorization, session, MFA, token, crypto, signature, webhook-verification and sandbox code.

- STALE: main already does it, with evidence.
- OPEN: needs a product decision or a design. One line says why.
