# Triage pass 2: open issues 0183 to 1199

Scope: the 210 open issues numbered 183 to 1199 (pull requests excluded). Each issue body was read. Checks against `origin/main` (commit `da03ba4`, fetched for this pass) are cited as `path:line`.

Classes:

- **CHEAP**: mechanical and periphery-safe. Does not touch the forbidden areas (namespace, identity, blob, secret, fence, `pkg/dustpan/dustpan.go`, baseline SQL, `openapi.json`, auth, crypto, signature, webhook, sandbox).
- **SIZED**: not cheap, but a spec can be written. Size: S under 50 lines, M under 200, L more.
- **STALE**: main already does it, with evidence.
- **OPEN**: product or design question, missing design, or blocked. One line says why.

Sizes are estimates from the body and the code on main. They are not measured.

Each CHEAP and SIZED entry has a three-line spec: Files, Change, Acceptance.
