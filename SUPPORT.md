# Support

OpenDustpan is pre-alpha. Support is through GitHub issues only. No paid
support and no promised response time exist.

## Where to ask

- **A bug**: open an issue. Give the server version
  (`opendustpan --version`), the CLI and its version, the database, the
  commands you ran, and what you saw.
- **A feature or a question about the design**: open an issue. Say what you
  want to do and why.
- **A security problem**: do not open an issue. Follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Search the open and closed issues first. Your question may have an answer.

## Before you ask

- Read the [README](README.md), with its quickstart and its section "When not
  to use OpenDustpan".
- Read the [guides](docs/guides/) and the
  [configuration reference](docs/reference/configuration.md).

## Vendor support

OpenDustpan is not affiliated with Pulumi Corporation, the OpenTofu project
or HashiCorp. Do not ask them for support with OpenDustpan.
