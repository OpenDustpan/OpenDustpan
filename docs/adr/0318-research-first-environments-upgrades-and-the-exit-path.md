## Context

## Decision

1. **Environments and promotion (owner, 2026-10-18).** The owner said: "Environments and promotion: research first".
2. **Upgrades (owner, 2026-10-18).** The owner said: "Upgrades: a 'versions in use' report". The owner said also: "plus an action that starts write-back jobs or change orders". The owner said also: "a namespace can turn it off".
3. **Ordered rollout (owner, 2026-10-18).** The owner said: "Ordered rollout across environments: later (with run triggers, after the environments research)".
4. **The exit path (owner, 2026-10-18).** The owner said: "Backup format spec: later". The owner said also: "Start planning and research for a CLI command that dumps a backup". The owner said also: "into something that can rebuild a Pulumi or OpenTofu state (exit path)". The owner said also: "Owner does not know yet how this works: research task".

## Consequences

- (PM, for the owner): RFC 55 and RFC 56 stay the place for the research.
