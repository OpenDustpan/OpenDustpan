# 331. Environment promotion is in milestone 6

Status: accepted · 2026-11-06. Decided by the project owner on 6 November 2026

Changes:

- Extends ADR 318 decisions 1 and 3: the research of decision 1 is RFC 55, and the ordered rollout of decision 3 stays later.

## Context

On 1 November 2026 the owner decided that no feature gets cut from milestones 4 to 6. [ADR 318](0318-research-first-environments-upgrades-and-the-exit-path.md) decides "research first" for environments and promotion, and puts the ordered rollout later.

On 6 November 2026, about 13:55 UTC, the owner wrote: "All are approved by me as proposed. RFC55 on 'no feature gets cut'."

## Decision

Decided by the project owner on 6 November 2026.

1. **RFC 55 is in milestone 6 (owner).** Environment promotion is in milestone 6. The owner rule of 1 November 2026 says that no feature gets cut.
2. **RFC 55 is the research (owner).** [RFC 55](../rfc/0055-environment-promotion.md) is the research for this feature.
3. **The ordered rollout stays later (owner).** The ordered rollout stays later, as ADR 318 decision 3 says.

## Consequences

- This ADR does not accept the proposals of RFC 55.
